| [bluestarindia.com](https://www.bluestarindia.com/products/commercial-refrigeration) | Blue Star, India | Indian market leader, multi-category conglomerate |

Three inner page types were also pulled apart, because a page list is useless
without knowing what sits *inside* a page:

- a product **category** page (True, Reach-In Refrigeration)
- a product **detail** page (Beverage-Air, model CT2HC-1S)
- a **support hub** (True, `/support`)

Each section below carries a **convergence count** — how many of the five sites
have it. A section at 5/5 is table stakes. A section at 1/5 is one company's pet
project and you can safely ignore it.

### Three findings that should drive the whole build

**1. Nobody shows a price.** Not one of the five. Every single path through every
site terminates in *request a quote*, *find a dealer*, or *contact us*. The
conversion event for this entire category of website is **an enquiry, not a
checkout**. Design every page backwards from that one action, and drop any idea
of a "build & price" configurator.

**2. The support sprawl is a trap.** True has ~25 distinct URLs under
`/support` — serial lookup, four separate warranty claim forms, parts order
tracking, work instructions by part number. Traulsen has ~15. Those exist because
they have millions of cabinets in the field and decades of service history.
**You have none of that yet.** Copy that structure and you ship twenty empty
pages, which reads as a dead company. One honest support page beats twenty stubs.

**3. The product detail page is the actual product.** Beverage-Air's model page
carries nine downloadable resources (spec sheet, manual, brochure, video,
warranty statement, Revit, three AutoCAD views) plus an eleven-row spec table.
That is the page a chef, a kitchen consultant and a purchase manager all land on
from Google. Spend your effort there, not on homepage animation.

## 4. Section-by-section specification

This is the main deliverable. For each section: what goes in it, why it is
valid for the user, and the verdict.

---

### 1. Home Section

**Contents**

1. **Utility bar** — phone number, `Get a Quote`, language/region if relevant. Sticky.
2. **Primary navigation** — Products · Industries · Service & Support · About · Contact. Five items, no more. Products opens a mega-menu listing all six categories with a thumbnail each.
3. **Hero** — one strong claim plus one photograph of equipment in a real kitchen. Two buttons: `Explore Products` (primary) and `Request a Quote` (secondary). Do not use a five-slide carousel; users see slide one and leave.
4. **Shop by product type** — a six-card grid, one per category, each card = photo + category name + one-line description + model count ("18 models"). This is the single highest-traffic element on the page after the nav.
5. **Shop by industry** — a six-card rail: Restaurants & QSR · Bakery & Patisserie · Supermarket & Retail · Hotels & Banqueting · Healthcare & Pharma · Ice Cream & Beverage.
6. **Why us** — four or five proof tiles, each a number, not an adjective: years in business · models in range · cities with service engineers · installations commissioned · warranty terms.
7. **Trusted by** — client logo strip. Western leads with this and it is the cheapest credibility on the page. B2B buyers scan logos before they read copy.
8. **Featured / newest models** — three to six product cards linking straight to detail pages.
9. **Service promise** — one band: pan-India service network, AMC available, genuine spares, response-time commitment.
10. **Enquiry CTA band** — form or button, plus phone and WhatsApp.
11. **Footer** — product categories · industries · company · support · contact block with full address and GST · social icons · legal links.

**User value** — the home page has one job: route the visitor to the right
category in a single click and prove you are a real company. Items 4, 5 and 7
do that. Everything else on this list is support for those three.

**Verdict: Must-have.** 5/5 convergence.

---

### 2. Products Section (catalogue hub)

**Contents**

- One-paragraph intro covering the whole range.
- **Six category cards**, mapped 1:1 to the Hoshizaki refrigeration catalogue (see §5) — photo, name, description, "View N models".
- A secondary row for adjacent lines you also carry (ice machines, dishwashers, water coolers) if applicable.
- **Filter / search entry point** — "Looking for something specific?" with the facet set from §6.
- `Download full product catalogue (PDF)` — one button. This is what distributors and consultants actually want.
- Enquiry CTA.

**User value** — this is the page that answers "do you even make the thing I
need?" in under five seconds. All five sites have it and all five structure it
as a category grid, because a flat list of 300 model codes is unusable.

**Verdict: Must-have.** 5/5 convergence.

---

### 3. Product Category Section (× 6 templates)

**Contents**

- Category hero: name, one photograph, two or three sentences on what the
  category is for. True's copy is the model to follow — *"Flexible. Adaptable.
  Reliably cold. True upright reach-ins have been the chef's companion in
  commercial kitchens for over 40 years."* Concrete, not corporate.
- **Sub-type blocks** — within Reach-In, that is Refrigerator / Freezer /
  Dual-Temp / Convertible, each with a two-line description and a
  `View all` link. This is exactly how True and Beverage-Air split theirs.
- **Model grid** — card per model: photo, model code, capacity, doors,
  temperature range, `View details` + `Enquire`.
- **Filter sidebar** — the facets from §6, applied to this category only.
- **Comparison table** — the key specs of every model in the category, side by
  side, in one scrollable table. None of the five do this well and it is the
  easiest place to beat them.
- "Not sure which model?" → link to the enquiry form with the category pre-filled.
- Related industries links.

**User value** — the buyer knows they need an undercounter unit but not which
one. This page narrows six to one. The comparison table is the fastest path to
that, which is why it is worth adding even though competitors lack it.

**Verdict: Must-have.** 5/5 convergence.

---

### 4. Product Detail Section (× one per model)

**The most important page on the site.** Organic search lands here, not on the
home page. Specification below is modelled on Beverage-Air's, which is the best
of the five.

**Contents**

1. **Image gallery** — 4–6 photos: closed, open with product loaded, interior, controls, in-situ. Plus a line drawing with dimensions.
2. **Model code + full name** as the H1 — e.g. *HR-78MA-S · Reach-In Refrigerator, Two Section, Solid Door*.
3. **At-a-glance block** — capacity (litres **and** cu. ft.), temperature range (°C), exterior W×D×H (**mm**), doors/sections, refrigerant, power.
4. **Primary CTAs** — `Request a Quote` and `Download Spec Sheet`, both above the fold. No price.
5. **Cabinet construction** — bullet list: exterior/interior material and grade, insulation type and thickness, door type and reversibility, lock, shelves (count + finish), castors, handle.
6. **Refrigeration system** — bullet list: refrigerant and compliance, compressor type, defrost method, evaporator coating, controller, temperature uniformity.
7. **Full specification table** — the eleven-row minimum Beverage-Air uses: model number · exterior dimensions · interior dimensions · exterior construction · interior construction · doors · shelves · net capacity · hinge location · compressor location · door type. Add for India: voltage/frequency/phase · power consumption (W) · ambient/climate class · net and gross weight · energy rating.
8. **Downloads block** — spec sheet PDF · installation & operation manual PDF · brochure PDF · warranty statement PDF · CAD (DWG front/plan/side) · Revit/BIM · product video. Beverage-Air ships all nine per model; even four is strong.
9. **Warranty** — stated plainly on the page, not buried. True leads with "7-year labour, parts and compressor" as a headline feature because it closes deals.
10. **Optional accessories** — shelves, castors, drawer kits, door locks.
11. **Suitable for** — industry tags linking back to industry pages.
12. **Related / comparable models** — three cards, including the next size up and down.
13. **Enquiry form** inline at the bottom, model pre-filled.

**User value** — three different people use this page: the chef checks capacity
and layout, the kitchen consultant needs the CAD and the drawing, the purchase
manager needs warranty, power rating and spec sheet to put in a tender. Serving
all three is what makes the enquiry arrive. Omitting the spec sheet PDF is the
single most common reason a B2B refrigeration enquiry does not happen.

**Verdict: Must-have. Build this template first and best.** 5/5 convergence.

---

### 5. Industries / Applications Section

**Contents**

- One page with a section per industry (not six separate pages in v1 — one
  well-built page outperforms six thin ones):
  - **Restaurants, QSR & Cloud Kitchens** — undercounters, worktops, prep tables, reach-ins
  - **Bakery, Patisserie & Confectionery** — pastry cabinets, display showcases, deep freezers
  - **Supermarket & Retail** — visi coolers, glass-door merchandisers, open-air cases
  - **Hotels, Banqueting & Institutional** — walk-ins, bulk reach-ins, ice machines
  - **Healthcare & Pharma** — medical refrigerators, ILR, vaccine and ultra-low freezers
  - **Ice Cream, Dairy & Beverage** — deep freezers, bottle coolers, milk coolers
- Each section: one photo, two or three sentences on the cooling problem that
  industry has, three to four recommended product links, and one installation
  reference if you have one.
- Enquiry CTA per section, with the industry pre-filled.

**User value** — buyers self-identify by business type, not by equipment
category. A bakery owner does not search "undercounter refrigerator"; they
search "bakery display cabinet". This page captures that intent and translates
it into product. It also doubles your long-tail search surface at low cost.

**Verdict: Must-have**, despite only 3/5 convergence — and that is a
considered disagreement with the matrix. True invests heavily here (fourteen
pages across `/concept-solutions` and `/true-retail`), which tells you it works.
Beverage-Air's omission is a gap, not a signal.

### 6. About Us Section

**Contents**

- **Who we are** — one honest paragraph. Years in business, what you make, where.
- **Numbers** — years · models · installations · cities served · plant area · employees. Western's "45+ nations" and True's "80-year anniversary" both work because they are specific.
- **Manufacturing / infrastructure** — plant photographs, capacity, automation, in-house processes. Western and Blue Star both give this its own page; fold it in here as a section until you have enough to split it out.
- **Quality & certifications** — ISO, BIS, CE, NSF, energy ratings, refrigerant compliance. Logos plus one line each. In Indian B2B tenders this section is frequently the deciding document.
- **R&D / engineering** — testing labs, design capability. 3/5 of the sites have this.
- **Sustainability** — natural refrigerants (R290/R600a), energy efficiency, e-waste compliance. One section, not a separate page. Hoshizaki's own positioning leans on early natural-refrigerant adoption; inherit that.
- **Partnerships / brand association** — Western's whole credibility pitch is "a proud member of Hoshizaki Corporation". If you have an equivalent relationship, say it here and on the home page.
- **Leadership** — optional, two or three people maximum.
- Links out to Careers and Contact.

**User value** — nobody buys a ₹2–10 lakh cabinet with a ten-year service life
from a company they cannot verify. This page exists to de-risk the purchase.
Certifications and manufacturing photos do that; a mission statement does not.

**Verdict: Must-have.** 5/5 convergence. Keep it to **one page** — the five
sites fragment this across up to six pages (history, sustainability, R&D,
manufacturing, governance, group companies) and none of it is better for it.

---

### 7. Service & Support Section

**Contents**

- **Call us first** — service helpline, hours, email, WhatsApp. Put it at the
  very top. True opens its support page with exactly this and it is correct.
- **Installation & commissioning** — what you do, lead time, what the customer prepares.
- **AMC & preventive maintenance** — plans offered, what each covers, how to ask for a quote.
- **Genuine spare parts** — how to order, availability commitment. Link to a parts enquiry form, not a store.
- **Warranty** — terms by product line, what is covered, what voids it, how to claim. One table.
- **Service network** — list or map of cities with engineers. Even a plain city list is useful; 4/5 sites have a locator of some kind.
- **Service request form** — model code, serial number, location, issue, contact. Four fields plus a dropdown.
- **FAQ** — ten to fifteen real questions: lead time, pricing, installation charges, who can service it, voltage requirements, warranty transfer.
- Link to Downloads for manuals.

**User value** — commercial refrigeration is bought on after-sales confidence.
A restaurant losing a cabinet on a Friday night loses stock and a weekend of
trade. This page answers "what happens when it breaks" before the purchase, and
it is the page existing customers use after. It is as much a sales page as a
service page.

**Verdict: Must-have, as ONE page.** 5/5 convergence. **Explicitly do not**
replicate True's twenty-five-URL support tree — see §9.

---

### 8. Contact & Enquiry Section

**Contents**

- **Enquiry form** — the primary conversion point of the whole site. Fields:
  name · company · phone · email · city · enquiry type (Product enquiry / Quote
  request / Service / Spare parts / Dealership / Careers) · product or model
  (pre-filled when arriving from a product page) · quantity · message.
  Nine fields maximum; every extra field costs you leads.
- **Direct channels** — sales phone, service phone, email, WhatsApp click-to-chat. India-specific and genuinely used.
- **Offices** — registered office and plant, full address, embedded map, GSTIN.
- **Regional sales contacts** — name, region, phone, if you have them.
- **Become a dealer / distributor** — separate short form. Western, True and Blue Star all carry a partner-intake route; it is cheap and it is how you grow coverage.
- Business hours.

**User value** — this is where the money is. Every other page on the site is a
funnel into this form. Offer phone and WhatsApp alongside it, because a large
share of Indian B2B buyers will not fill a form at all.

**Verdict: Must-have.** 5/5 convergence.

---

### 9. Downloads Section

**Contents**

- **Full product catalogue** (PDF) — the hero download.
- **Spec sheets** — filterable by category, one per model.
- **Installation & operation manuals** — by model.
- **Brochures** — by range.
- **Certificates** — ISO, BIS, CE, test reports.
- **CAD / Revit / BIM** — by model.
- Optional email capture on download. Optional — do not gate the spec sheets.

**User value** — kitchen consultants, architects and tender teams need
documents, not web pages. 3/5 of the sites invest here (True's
`/support/resource-center` and `/support/manuals`, Beverage-Air's `/tech-manuals`,
Traulsen's `/traulsen-resources`). Western has none, and it is a visible weakness
in the closest comparable site. This page is also the cheapest to build — it is
a filtered list over one table — which makes it the best effort-to-value ratio
on the whole site.

**Verdict: Must-have.** Every file on it is also linked from its product page;
this is the index for people who arrive looking for documents.

---

### 10. Privacy Policy + Terms of Use Sections

**Contents** — standard: data collected, cookies, form data use, retention,
contact for data requests; terms of use, product-image disclaimer,
specification-change-without-notice clause (every one of the five has this),
IP notice. Add an e-waste handling note for India, as Blue Star does.

**User value** — low direct value, non-negotiable in practice. 5/5 convergence.

**Verdict: Must-have in the footer.** Two thin pages.

## Phase 2 — add when there is real content

### 11. Dealer / Where to Buy Section

**Contents** — searchable list or map by state and city; dealer name, address,
phone, product lines carried; "no dealer near you? contact us direct"; link to
the become-a-dealer form.

**User value** — 4/5 convergence, and in India the dealer *is* the buying
channel. **Hold it back only until you have dealers to list** — an empty map is
worse than no map. Promote it to v1 the day the list exists.

---

### 12. Why Us / Technology Section

**Contents** — one page, three to five genuine differentiators, each with a
diagram or photo: temperature uniformity and airflow design · natural
refrigerants and energy consumption · stainless grade and build quality ·
controller and diagnostics · serviceability. Traulsen runs five of these pages
(`/technology/temperature-airflow`, `/smart-control`,
`/stayclear-condenser-coil`, `/ez-clean-gasket`, `/ezclean-trayracks`) and
Beverage-Air built a whole explainer around Cross Temp.

**User value** — moves you off price competition. Only worth building when the
claims are real and you have the test data; invented differentiators are worse
than none.

**Verdict: Should-have.** 3/5 convergence.

---

### 13. Installations / Case Studies Section

**Contents** — per project: client (with permission), city, industry, equipment
supplied, photographs, one-line outcome. Grid filterable by industry.

**User value** — the strongest trust asset available to a refrigeration brand,
and the most under-used: only Blue Star has it (`/project-gallery`). A bakery
owner seeing three bakeries you have fitted out is worth more than any amount of
copy. **Start photographing installations now**, even before the page exists.

**Verdict: Should-have, high value.** 1/5 convergence — which is the
opportunity, not a reason to skip it.

---

### 14. News & Insights Section

**Contents** — one combined feed, not three. Product launches, trade-show
presence, and genuinely useful how-to articles. Western's blog is the model to
copy: *"Why crystal-clear ice is the secret weapon for high-end hospitality"*,
*"Low-height drawer series: cook above, chill below"* — each one is a product
page in disguise, and each is tagged to the product it sells.

**User value** — SEO and credibility. Worthless if it goes stale. Only start
it if you can publish once a month for a year; a blog whose newest post is
fourteen months old actively damages trust.

**Verdict: Should-have, conditional on commitment.** 4/5 convergence.

---

### 15. Careers Section

**Contents** — why work here, two or three photos, current openings (role,
location, experience, apply link), application form with CV upload, HR email.

**User value** — 5/5 convergence, so it is expected, but it serves recruitment
rather than customers. A single page that says "write to careers@…" is an
acceptable v1.

**Verdict: Should-have.** Do not let it compete with the product pages for effort.

## 5. Product taxonomy, mapped to the Hoshizaki range

Hoshizaki's refrigeration page lists six key products. Map your categories 1:1
onto them, so the catalogue stays honest to what you can actually supply.

| # | Your category | Hoshizaki source family | Sub-types to list |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Reach-In Refrigerators & Freezers** | Reach-in Refrigerator | Refrigerator · Freezer · Dual-temp · Convertible. One/two/three section; full or half doors; stainless or glass |
| 2 | **Undercounter & Worktop Refrigeration** | Undercounter Refrigerator | Undercounter · Worktop · Drawer units · Chef base. Refrigerator/freezer/dual-temp |
| 3 | **Glass Door Refrigerators & Visi Coolers** | Glass Door Refrigerator / Visi Cooler | Visi coolers · Countertop units · Full-height merchandisers · Compact units |
| 4 | **Display Cases & Showcases** | Sushi Display Case | Pastry & confectionery cabinets · Deli/sushi cases · Curved-glass display · High-humidity cases |
| 5 | **Deep Freezers** | Deep Freezer | Hard-top · Glass-top · Ultra-low temperature (to −60 °C) |
| 6 | **Walk-In Cold Rooms** | Walk-in Refrigerator | Freezer · Refrigerator · High-humidity rooms |

**Direct quotes worth reusing in your category copy** (from Hoshizaki's own page):

> "From compact undercounter units to upright cabinets and glass-door
> merchandisers, our refrigeration solutions combine energy efficiency,
> usability, and low environmental impact."

> "We have led the industry with innovations such as the early adoption of

**Two notes on scope.** Hoshizaki marks Walk-in as Japan-only and Deep Freezer
as available in Japan / East & Southeast Asia / South Asia — so confirm regional
availability before publishing category 6. And Western, the Hoshizaki India arm,
sells well beyond these six: visi coolers, pastry and confectionery cabinets,
preparation tables, back bars, ice machines, dishwashers, glass washers, medical
refrigerators, water coolers, dispensers, supermarket freezers and vending
machines. **Ice machines in particular are Hoshizaki's flagship product
worldwide** — if you can carry them, they belong in the nav as a seventh
category, not buried.

## 6. Faceted filter specification

One facet set, used on the catalogue hub and every category page. Metric units
first — this is the most common localisation failure when copying US sites.

| Facet | Values |
|---|---|
| Product type | Refrigerator · Freezer · Dual-temp · Convertible |
| Configuration | Upright/reach-in · Undercounter · Worktop · Drawer · Glass door · Display case · Chest · Walk-in |
| Sections / doors | 1 · 2 · 3 · 4+ |
| Door type | Solid stainless · Half door · Full glass · Sliding · Drawer |
| Capacity | Litres (range slider), cu. ft. shown secondary |
| Temperature range | +2 to +8 °C · −18 to −22 °C · −40 °C · −60 °C · dual |
| Exterior width | mm (range slider) |
| Refrigerant | R290 · R600a · R134a · R404A |
| Finish | SS 304 · SS 430 · Powder-coated |
| Power | 230 V single phase · 415 V three phase |
| Industry | the six from §5 |

True is the only one of the five with real faceted search (`/advanced-search`).
With 300+ model codes this stops being a nice-to-have and becomes the difference
between a usable catalogue and an unusable one.

```
│   └── [Ice Machines]                    → model pages
├── Industries            (one page, six anchored sections)
├── Service & Support     (one page: helpline, AMC, spares, warranty, network, FAQ)
├── Downloads             (catalogue, spec sheets, manuals, certificates, CAD)
├── About Us              (company, plant, certifications, R&D, sustainability)
└── Contact               (enquiry form, offices, dealership enquiry)

Footer: Privacy · Terms · Careers · Sitemap · Social
Persistent: Get a Quote (button) · phone · WhatsApp
```

**Five primary nav items.** The five audited sites run between five and nine;
the ones at nine are harder to use, not richer. Everything a visitor needs is
within two clicks.

## 8. Build order

| Phase | Build | Why this order |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product Detail template + 6 Category pages + Products hub | This is the site. Organic search lands here, and the enquiry is made here |
| 2 | Contact & Enquiry, Service & Support, Downloads | Closes the loop on every product page CTA |
| 3 | Home, Industries, About | The framing. Worth less than people assume — build it after the catalogue works |
| 4 | Privacy, Terms, Careers | Required, thin |
| 5 | Dealer locator, Installations, Why Us, News | Add as the content to fill them becomes real |

The instinct is to build the home page first. Resist it. A beautiful home page
over an empty catalogue converts nothing; a plain catalogue with complete spec
sheets converts immediately.

## 9. What to deliberately leave out, and why

| Leave out | Found on | Why not |
|---|---|---|
| Serial number lookup | True | Needs a serial database and an installed base. You have neither |
| Four separate warranty claim forms | True | One service request form covers it |
| Parts e-commerce + order tracking | True, Beverage-Air | A whole storefront with inventory and payments. Parts enquiry form instead |
| Account login / cart | Beverage-Air | No prices means no cart |
| "Build & price" configurator | — | Not one of the five shows a price. Do not be the first |
| Investor relations | Blue Star | Listed-company obligation. Not yours |
| Governance / group companies | Blue Star | Conglomerate structure. Noise for a single brand |
| Separate Sustainability page | Western, True, Blue Star | A section inside About Us. A thin standalone page reads as greenwashing |
| Separate R&D and Factories pages | Western, Blue Star | Sections inside About Us |
| Separate press-release feed | Traulsen, Blue Star | One News feed. Traulsen's twelve press releases are mostly parent-company news |
| White paper library | Traulsen | Needs real research |
| Video library as its own page | Beverage-Air, Traulsen | Embed videos on the product pages where they sell |
| 6 separate industry pages | True (14 of them) | One page with six anchored sections. Split later if traffic justifies it |
| Multi-slide hero carousel | Western, Traulsen | Measured conversion on carousel slides 2+ is close to zero |
| Horizontal-scroll showcase | current `index.html` §6 | High build cost, high accessibility risk, no sales function |

**The rule:** every page you ship must have content on day one. Nine complete
pages beat twenty-five pages where eighteen say "coming soon".

## 10. India-specific requirements the US sites will not teach you

1. **WhatsApp click-to-chat** on every page. Not optional for Indian B2B.
2. **Metric first** — litres and mm primary, cu. ft. and inches secondary.
3. **230 V / 415 V, 50 Hz** power specs, and ambient/climate class rated for Indian summer conditions.
4. **GSTIN** in the footer and on the contact page.
5. **No prices**, but a fast quote path — and state your typical response time.
6. **Service city list**, explicitly. "Pan-India service" without a city list is not believed.
7. **BIS / ISI** alongside ISO and CE in certifications.
8. **E-waste handling note**, as Blue Star carries.
9. **Dealer and distributor enquiry form** — your growth channel.
10. **Page weight** — a large share of traffic will be on mobile data. Compress the photography; this is a photo-heavy catalogue.

## 12. Summary

**Build nine pages.** Home · Products hub · six Category templates · Product
Detail template · Industries · About · Service & Support · Contact ·
Downloads, plus Privacy and Terms in the footer.

**Spend 60% of the effort on the Product Detail template.** Complete spec
tables, metric-first units, and a downloadable spec sheet on every model.

**Make the enquiry the only conversion event.** No prices, no cart. Phone and
WhatsApp beside every form.

**Add five more pages later** — Dealer locator, Installations, Why Us, News,
Careers — each on the day its content actually exists, not before.
